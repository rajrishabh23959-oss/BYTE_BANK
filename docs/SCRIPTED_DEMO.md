1. **Tab 1: Concurrency Race Condition**:
   - Click **"Execute Concurrent Race"**.
   - Watch two simultaneous database connections attempt to transfer 0.8 BTC from a 1.0 BTC balance.

   - Final verified balance: **exactly 0.20000000 BTC**. Zero negative balance, zero lost update.
2. **Tab 2: Idempotency Verification**:
   - Click **"Scenario B: Payload Conflict"**.

3. **Tab 3: Crash Rollback & Autonomous Logging**:
   - Click **"Trigger Transaction Crash"**.
   - Show that the main transfer rolls back cleanly with zero balance mutation, but the failed-attempt transaction and audit log **survive via autonomous connection logging**.
4. **Tab 4: Reconciliation Drift & Compensating Resolution**:
